Memory 1 — The Missed Bus

One afternoon, Friend A and I missed a bus after staying late to finish
a class project. We walked to the stop carrying a large sheet of paper
that kept folding in the breeze. I tried to hold it flat against my bag
while Friend A read the project headings aloud. A crowded bus arrived,
so we decided to wait for the next one. We listened to traffic and
watched people join the line behind us. When our bus finally came, I
tucked the bent paper under my arm and climbed aboard after Friend A.

Memory 2 — The Dripping Tap

One weekend morning, Housemate C and I noticed that the kitchen tap
would not close properly. Every few seconds, it dropped water into a
metal bowl underneath. I was meant to study, but the steady sound kept
interrupting me. Housemate C suggested wrapping the tap with a strip of
cloth, which slowed the drip without stopping it. I wrote “call a
plumber” on a scrap of paper and left it by the sink. We ate breakfast
standing by the counter because the table was covered with notebooks
and clean cups. Before leaving, I emptied the bowl, folded the damp
cloth over the tap, and shut the kitchen door.
